Admissions Open

Admissions open for Classes Nursery to VIII & XI.

Guidelines for Filling the Online Application Form

Kidees Info Park, Peth is committed to providing quality education in a disciplined and nurturing environment. Parents are requested to carefully review the following guidelines before submitting the admission form.

  1. Ensure the name of the child and parents matches government documents such as Aadhaar Card and Birth Certificate.
  2. Please read all rules and regulations carefully before filling the online application form.
  3. Each field in the application form is compulsory and must be filled accurately.
  4. Verify age eligibility criteria before applying for the respective class.
  5. Age limits will be strictly followed as per school norms.
  6. Transfer/Leaving Certificate details must match the application form details.
  7. Submit copies of Aadhaar card of the child and parent (if available).
  8. Incomplete applications may be rejected without prior notice.

The school reserves the right to grant or deny admission based on eligibility criteria, document verification, and seat availability.

Age Criteria

Admission is granted strictly as per age eligibility norms prescribed by the institution. The minimum age requirement must be fulfilled as of the academic year start date.

  • Nursery: As per academic session guidelines
  • Primary & Secondary: Age appropriate to previous academic completion
  • Class XI: Based on completion of Class X from recognized board

Subjects offered in Class XI

The institution offers multiple streams for higher secondary education:

  • Science Stream – Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Mathematics
  • Commerce Stream – Accountancy, Economics, Business Studies
  • Arts Stream – History, Political Science, Sociology

Subject combinations may vary depending on board regulations and availability.

Fee Structure

The fee structure is determined according to the class level and facilities provided. Detailed fee information can be obtained from the school administrative office.

  • Admission Fee
  • Tuition Fee
  • Development Fee
  • Examination Fee (if applicable)

Students Transfer Details

Students seeking transfer from another institution must submit:

  • Original Transfer Certificate
  • Previous Academic Report Card
  • Birth Certificate
  • Aadhaar Copy
