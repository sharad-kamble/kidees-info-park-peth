About Our School

Welcome to Kidees Info Park

Kidees Info Park is a progressive CBSE English Medium School dedicated to delivering quality education through innovation, discipline, and value-based learning. Our institution believes that education goes beyond textbooks and focuses on nurturing creativity, confidence, and character in every child.

We provide a stimulating academic environment where students are encouraged to explore ideas, develop analytical thinking, and build strong communication skills. Our experienced faculty members adopt modern teaching methodologies that make learning interactive, enjoyable, and meaningful.

At Kidees Info Park , students grow academically, socially, emotionally, and physically through balanced exposure to academics, sports, cultural activities, leadership programs, and technology-enabled education.
