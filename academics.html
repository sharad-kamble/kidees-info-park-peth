Assessment & Evaluation System

A balanced and transparent evaluation process focused on continuous improvement and overall student development.

Our Evaluation Approach
  • Continuous assessments through class tests, projects, and assignments.
  • Periodic written examinations to measure subject understanding.
  • Observation-based evaluation for participation, discipline, and teamwork.
  • Skill-based grading for co-curricular and practical activities.
Grading & Promotion Criteria
  • Performance is reflected through a structured grading scale to encourage steady academic progress.
  • Students are required to secure a minimum qualifying score in both internal assessments and written examinations.
  • Regular attendance is essential for academic continuity and eligibility for class promotion.
  • Overall conduct and participation are considered as part of holistic evaluation.

At Kidees Info Park Peth, assessment is not just about scores. It is a structured process designed to identify strengths, improve learning gaps, and nurture confident, capable learners.
